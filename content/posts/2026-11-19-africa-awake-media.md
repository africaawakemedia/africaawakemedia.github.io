# AFRICA AWAKE MEDIA



## Taken Darasi: Muhimmancin Ilimi a Ci gaban Al'umma

*The Importance of Education in Community Development*



### Gabatarwa

Ilimi shi ne ginshiƙin ci gaban kowace al'umma. Yana taimaka wa mutane su fahimci duniya, su magance matsaloli, su kuma inganta rayuwarsu.



### Me ya sa ilimi yake da muhimmanci?

1. Yana rage jahilci.

2. Yana samar da ƙwarewa da hanyoyin samun aikin yi.

3. Yana taimakawa wajen ci gaban tattalin arziki.

4. Yana ƙarfafa zaman lafiya da fahimtar juna.

5. Yana taimaka wa matasa su gina makoma mai kyau.



### English Summary

Education is the foundation of community development. It empowers people with knowledge, skills, and opportunities to improve their lives and contribute positively to society.



### Kammalawa

Dole ne mu ba ilimi muhimmanci, mu ƙarfafa yara da matasa su yi karatu, domin su ne shugabanni da masu gina al'ummar gobe.
