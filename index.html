WHO WE ARE

Our past is a source of knowledge. Our future is a shared responsibility.

Africa Awake Media is a multilingual media project dedicated to African history, biographies, public awareness, critical thinking and constructive conversations about the continent’s future.

We aim to tell human stories with context, respect and evidence — celebrating achievement while examining injustice and difficult chapters honestly.
