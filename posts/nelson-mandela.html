Farkon rayuwa

An haifi Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela a Mvezo, Gabashin Afirka ta Kudu, ranar 18 ga Yuli 1918. Ya girma cikin al'adun Thembu, ya yi karatu a makarantu daban-daban, daga baya ya shiga harkar shari'a da siyasa.

Gwagwarmayar adawa da wariyar launin fata

Mandela ya shiga African National Congress (ANC), inda ya taimaka wajen shirya fafutukar adawa da tsarin apartheid wanda ya ware mutane bisa launin fata. A 1964 aka yanke masa hukuncin ɗaurin rai da rai a shari'ar Rivonia.

Shekarun gidan yari da sakin sa

Ya shafe shekaru 27 a kurkuku, mafi yawansu a tsibirin Robben. An sake shi a ranar 11 ga Fabrairu 1990. Bayan sakin sa ya shiga tattaunawar kawo ƙarshen mulkin wariyar launin fata.

Shugabanci da sulhu

A 1993 Mandela da F. W. de Klerk sun sami kyautar Nobel ta zaman lafiya. A 1994 aka zaɓe shi shugaban Afirka ta Kudu a zaɓen dimokuraɗiyya na farko da kowa ya shiga. Ya jaddada sulhu da gina ƙasa, duk da cewa ƙalubalen rashin daidaito bai ƙare nan take ba.

Abin da tarihi ya koya

Mandela ya rasu a 2013. Tarihinsa yana nuna ƙarfin dagewa da neman adalci, amma kuma yana tunatar da mu cewa gina zaman lafiya yana buƙatar tattaunawa, cibiyoyi masu adalci da aiki mai ɗorewa.
